Why It Matters

Your Floor Is Built to Last — Care Makes Sure It Does

Every floor we install starts with full diamond grinding and finishes with our epoxy-polyaspartic/polyurea hybrid basecoat — a system engineered to handle hot tires, dropped tools, chemical spills, and years of daily traffic. That durability is real, but "low-maintenance" doesn't mean "no-maintenance." Regular care is what separates a floor that still looks brand new after ten years from one that's dull, scratched, and tired-looking after two.

The biggest threat to a coated floor isn't heavy use — it's grit. Sand, gravel, and dirt tracked in on tires and shoes act like sandpaper every time they're ground into the surface underfoot. Left alone, that constant micro-abrasion slowly wears down the topcoat's gloss and clarity, the same layer that gives your floor its chemical resistance and UV stability. A regular deep clean is what stops that wear before it starts — which is exactly what our maintenance plans are built around.

Maintenance also protects the coverage you're paying for. Our written Lifetime Warranty covers real coating failures — peeling and delamination under normal use — but it isn't a substitute for basic care. Damage caused by harsh chemicals, dragging heavy or sharp objects, or letting spills sit for weeks isn't a manufacturing defect, and it's not something any warranty in this industry covers. Staying on top of upkeep is a small habit that keeps both the floor and the warranty behind it fully intact.

Finally, care is what protects your investment's appearance — which, for most homeowners, is the whole point. Whether it's a showroom-quality garage floor, a finished basement, or a commercial space customers walk through every day, the reflective, flake-filled finish you paid for only looks that good if it's kept clean. Unlike bare concrete, tile grout, or wood, our coatings never need waxing, stripping, or resealing — they just need a bit of regular attention, whether that's a few minutes with a broom or a scheduled visit from our crew.

Between Visits

Everyday Care You Can Do Yourself

Whether or not you're on a plan, these habits go a long way between deep cleans.

✅ Do
  • Sweep or dust mop weekly to remove grit before it gets ground into the surface
  • Clean with warm water and a neutral pH cleaner between deep cleans
  • Wipe up fluid spills — oil, antifreeze, transmission fluid — as soon as you notice them
  • Rinse off winter road salt and de-icer residue every week or two during salt season
  • Use felt pads or coasters under legs on frequently moved furniture or equipment
🚫 Don't
  • Use bleach, ammonia-based cleaners, vinegar, or citrus degreasers — they dull the topcoat over time
  • Drag heavy appliances or metal equipment across the floor without protection
  • Let standing water, salt brine, or chemical spills sit for days at a time
  • Use steel wool, abrasive scrub pads, or a pressure washer on its highest setting
  • Assume the floor never needs attention — a tough coating still needs basic upkeep
Common Questions

Maintenance Plan FAQ

Do I really need a maintenance plan for a coated floor?▾
Our epoxy-polyaspartic/polyurea hybrid basecoat system is built to be low-maintenance, not no-maintenance. Regular deep cleaning removes the grit that acts like sandpaper on the topcoat, keeps the floor looking showroom-new, and helps protect the coverage under your Lifetime Warranty.
What's included in a maintenance visit?▾
Every visit includes a deep surface clean using a neutral pH cleaner safe for your coating, removal of dirt, dust, salt residue, and light staining, and a quick inspection to catch any issues early.
Will skipping maintenance void my warranty?▾
Our written Lifetime Warranty covers genuine coating failures like peeling and yellowing. It doesn't require a paid plan, but basic upkeep is the customer's responsibility, and neglect or harsh chemical damage isn't a covered failure.
Can I just do maintenance myself instead of a plan?▾
Absolutely — sweeping weekly and cleaning with a neutral pH cleaner every few weeks goes a long way. Our plans are for homeowners and businesses who'd rather have a professional handle deep cleaning, salt removal, and inspections on a set schedule.
Do you offer plans for commercial spaces?▾
Yes. Warehouses, auto shops, churches, and other high-traffic commercial floors typically benefit most from our Quarterly Plan. Call for a custom quote based on square footage.
