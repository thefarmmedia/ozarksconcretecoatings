How It Works

Our Proven 5-Step Process

Every install follows the same meticulous process. No shortcuts. No skipped steps. That's why our floors last a lifetime.

1
Surface Prep

Diamond grinding removes all coatings, oils, and imperfections. Cracks filled and leveled. This step is everything.

2
Primer Coat

Specialized primer applied for maximum adhesion between the coating system and your concrete substrate.

3
Base Coat

High-performance polyaspartic base coat spread evenly across the floor creating a flawless, bonded foundation.

4
Decorative Layer

Color flakes, metallic pigments, or solid tones broadcast into the wet base. Your floor, your look.

5
Topcoat Seal

UV-stable, chemical-resistant topcoat locks it all in. Gorgeous, easy to maintain. Drive on it in 24 hours.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

View All FAQs →
How long does an epoxy garage floor last?▾
With professional-grade polyaspartic coatings and full diamond grinding prep, expect a lifetime of durability. Our system is backed by a written warranty covering peeling and delamination — see full coverage details.
Is polyaspartic better than regular epoxy?▾
We don't make you choose — we use an epoxy-polyaspartic/polyurea hybrid basecoat, the best of both worlds. It's UV stable, more flexible, and cures far faster than standard epoxy alone.
How long before I can drive on my new floor?▾
Your floor is typically ready for foot traffic within hours and vehicle traffic within 24 hours. Most jobs are done in a single day start to finish.
Why do some epoxy floors peel and fail?▾
The #1 cause is skipping or cutting corners on surface preparation. Most cheap installers skip diamond grinding. We use full diamond grinding on every job — no exceptions.
How much does an epoxy garage floor cost in Missouri?▾
A typical 2-car garage (400–600 sq ft) ranges from $1,800–$3,500. We provide free quotes with honest pricing. Use our instant calculator to get an estimate.
Do you repair cracks and damage before coating?▾
Yes. All cracks and surface damage are filled and leveled as part of our standard prep process. We fix problems — we don't coat over them.
Do you serve areas outside Springfield?▾
Absolutely. We serve a full 75-mile radius around Springfield — including Nixa, Ozark, Branson, Republic, Joplin, Carthage, Neosho, Bolivar, Lebanon, and many more communities.
